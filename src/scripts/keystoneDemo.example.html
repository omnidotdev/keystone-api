Our craft

A ten-year argument with a roasting drum.

Ember started with one secondhand 3kg roaster, a notebook, and a stubborn belief that coffee should taste like somewhere, not like everywhere. A decade later we still roast by ear — listening for the crack, watching the color shift, pulling the batch the second it sings.

We buy direct from nine farms and pay above market every season, because the best cup starts long before the drum gets hot. Everything ships within 48 hours of roasting, in bags you can compost when the last scoop is gone.

  • Direct trade relationships, renewed every harvest.
  • Batch sizes capped at 12kg so nothing gets lost.
  • Roast-dated bags, never warehoused.
